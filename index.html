How beats and rap work together

1. The beat
A steady pulse, like a heartbeat. It keeps going the same speed.
2. The bar
One bar is 4 beats. Count: 1 2 3 4, then start again.
3. Words land on the beat
Each word or sound fits a beat.
I1
see2
my3
friends4
4. Kick and snare
Kick is a low boom. Snare is a sharp clap. Hats tick between them. Together they make the groove.
5. Speed
Speed is counted in BPM (beats per minute). Slow is about 70. Medium is about 90. Fast is 120 or more.

🧱 Pick one theme

Choose one idea for the song. Example: “My mum is my hero.” Keep each verse about that idea.

📖 Tell a story in 3 steps

1. Where am I?
2. What do I see?
3. How do I feel?
Use one step for each part of your verse.

🔗 Simile

Compare two things with “like” or “as”.
Example: “Fast like a train.”

✨ Metaphor

Say one thing IS another thing.
Example: “My street is a stage.”

💥 Punchline

A surprise at the end of a line.
Example: “I’m not asleep, I’m just resting my eyes for 12 hours.”

🎯 Hook first

Write the catchy part first. Keep it short. Make it easy to say again and again.

🏁 End on a good note

Finish with a line that feels like a full stop. Hopeful lines work well.
